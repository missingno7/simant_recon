# Window initialized views v36 — worker proposal

**Propose two minimum source-functional owners: `win_drawHooks[45]` (180 bytes) and `win_offsets[45]` (360 bytes), in the standalone provider `window-functional-provider45.c`.** Both are independently grounded in unconditional source writes. This resolves 540 bytes of minimum storage ownership without asserting historical maximal capacities. `WINDOW_INDEX_RESOURCE_CROSS_OWNER_LAYOUT_V36` must remain an open, independent-link-blocking gate. `win_colors` and `win_handles` remain unresolved. Every admission/review flag in the worker contract is false; parent is the sole acceptance writer.

The proposed definitions are exactly:

```c
struct Rect { int left,top,right,bottom; };
void (far * far win_drawHooks[45])(int phase);
struct Rect far win_offsets[45];
```

MSC600ax emits far COMDEF `{count:45, element_size:4, length:180}` for hooks and `{count:45, element_size:8, length:360}` for Rects, with no initializer/data contribution. No scalar aliases are needed. Integration should add this data-only provider, leaving every consumer TU intact. Complete `root:20E8` ownership drafts are isolation controls, not proposed canonical edits.

The lower spans are source-owned, independent of input-domain assumptions. `win_LoadAllWindows` clears exactly `0xb4` hook bytes and assigns all four signed16 Rect fields for `i=0..44` from `{0x8000,0x8000,0x8000,0x8000}`. Its optional `0x140` copy overwrites rows0..39 only; rows40..44 retain the sentinel. Original disassembly confirms `B4`, `CMP 2D/JL`, `140`, and the signed pointer/Rect indexing. `loadall-extraction.json` independently extracts the current canonical and effective generated whole-TU reset/copy statements and sentinel declaration; those relevant expressions are equal. The runtime fixture exercises those extracted effects with constructed signed test Rects, without executing the DB/font/remainder of the loader.

This is stronger than a conditional guessed capacity. Every reset needs all45 entries even when no resource is loaded; owning exactly that proven minimum is a functional representation choice. The observed installed window domain0..33, all seven source hook registrations (0,1,21,18,19,25,5), and intended lock-admitted indices0..40 fit. No proven legitimate access outside0..44 was established. The unrestricted source paths are retained in the separate gate, rather than being treated as bounds or being silently excluded from full integration. The proposal does **not** claim unconditional full-program integration or that the historical allocation ended after entry44.

Fresh full-object controls distinguish the scopes. The entire effective `root:20E8` compiles with extern dimensions45 and46 to identical nondebug code/data/publics/ordered fixups. Whole-module owner45 and owner46 drafts likewise preserve all those observables, changing only communal dimensions (180/360 versus184/368) and declaration/debug metadata.
