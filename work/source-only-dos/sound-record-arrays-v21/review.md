## Ownership evidence

`fd_50F6_0000` is a 56-row, six-byte far-record candidate. In `src/root/m277E.c`, `struct Voice` is a signed `int` followed by a 32-bit `long`; `f_277E_010A` copies both fields for every `i < 0x38`. Its original no-raw transcript, [context-f277e-010a.txt](context-f277e-010a.txt), shows stride 6, stores at offsets 0, 2, and 4, and the same 56-row bound. `src/root/m0000.c:f_0000_0429` independently visits all 56 rows during cleanup; [context-f0000-0429.txt](context-f0000-0429.txt) shows the matching six-byte stride and `i < 0x38`. Nine source instrument tables in `src/data/d55B3_00B8.c` are each declared `[56]`; eight are passed to the copy routine, and the ninth (`fd_55B3_16C2`) is a separate declared 56-row table without a tracked copy call. Other consumers view the same record as `int` plus a large-model/far data pointer (`Instr`), or `int` plus `int far *` (`Drv`). The provider uses a union of `long` and `char far *` at offset 2 so these distinct views share the observed six-byte representation without asserting source-level type identity.

`fd_50F6_4A4E` is a 33-row, six-byte far-record candidate. `src/root/m277E.c:f_277E_00AF` writes all six byte fields for `i < 0x21` (rows 0–32); [context-f277e-00af.txt](context-f277e-00af.txt) confirms stride 6 and those offsets. The writer view is six plain `char` fields, including `c3 = -1`; `src/root/m295C.c` independently uses six `unsigned char` fields at the same offsets. The probe retains these as separate compiler views. The nine source setup modes in `g_68B6` fill at most 30 rows: mode 8 is at most base 4 + 8 MIDI + 8 percussion + 10 drums; its alternate branch reaches at most 22. The other modes top out at 15. The base is set to 2, or 4 when the flag is active, in `f_277E_0000`. Consumers in `m295C.c` scan until the `type == 0` sentinel. The 33-row candidate is therefore based on the explicit complete reset span together with the source setup and sentinel behavior. Neither the adjacent `fd_50F6_4B14` label nor gap arithmetic contributes to the proposed count.

The source graph's registered data labels inside the candidate spans are only `fd_50F6_0000` at `50F6:0000` and `fd_50F6_4A4E` at `50F6:4A4E`. It contains no registered data aliases or interior labels for either span, no alternate-name source references, no base-address-taking escapes, and no numeric references to these candidate addresses. The only related name aliases are code entries `f_00DF_00E8 -> myBeginSound` and `f_00DF_0112 -> myBeginSoundReverse`; the former has 22 literal calls, and the latter has no source-name call. The source audit scans 127 canonical TUs plus all 29 strict-effective entries (156 unique paths), verifies their expected source hashes, and reports no source or strict-index hash issues. It also scans current report sources separately; the report currently has 160 TUs and remains incomplete. No original executable bytes were used.

## Index and separate layout contracts

The 156-path census finds 124 sound-entry callsites: 101 `myBeginSound` entries (100 literal calls plus the one wrapper forward), 22 literal calls through `f_00DF_00E8`, and one literal call to `myBeginSoundReverse`. Their tracked literal first arguments are 1–55; the reverse call is literal 32 and its wrapper forwards that value unchanged. There are no source address escapes or indirect entry assignments in the scanned graph. `myBeginSound` forwards to `f_295C_0367`; its other direct caller passes literal 1. The 56-row `fd_55B3_0A82` SfxNote table's device-index field ranges 0–54. The fixed 14-entry bank initializer in `src/data/d55B3_0064.c` is `{0,25,53,3,54,35,46,50,51,48,0,12,43,52}`, also within 0–54. The bank fields have no source writes; `m284A.c` copies them to the distinct `fd_50F6_4B8E` map.

`f_284A_0521` only writes `fd_50F6_4B8E[chan]`, not either candidate record array. Its only caller is case 12 in `f_284A_0537`; the input type is `(status & 0x70) >> 4`, hence 0–7, making that case unreachable. [context-f284a-0537.txt](context-f284a-0537.txt) preserves the original disassembly supporting that check. These call and resource-index observations bound the tracked source paths; they do not validate arbitrary resources or establish full sound safety. The separate pre-sound-init DB gate reported by the parent depends on zero initial `fd_50F6_4A4E[].type` sentinel state and zero initial `fd_50F6_0000[].kind` values: `f_295C_0391` scans until a zero type, and `f_0000_0429` reads the kind field in all 56 Voice rows. These readers do not read `fd_50F6_4A4C`; its use as a setup index is separate. This cross-wave context does not expand either owner claim.

## Compiler and linker measurements

The proposed uninitialized, code-less `SNDRECS` provider yields exactly two far COMDEFs under MSC 6.00AX `/AL /Os /Gs`: `_fd_50F6_0000` count 56 × element 6 = 336 bytes, and `_fd_50F6_4A4E` count 33 × element 6 = 198 bytes. The object has no publics, code, initialized segments, or other data segments. `sizeof`/`offsetof` controls cover the Voice, Instr (sample/byte/void pointer), Drv, and separate Chan views; Voice is 6 bytes with kind at 0 and payload at 2, and Chan fields occupy offsets 0–5.
